### 🪶 About Me

I'm a Software Engineering student who lives at the intersection of **AI, data, and clean backend systems**. My work is centered on turning raw data into decisions and repetitive workflows into automated pipelines — through Python, machine learning, and tools like FastAPI, LangChain, and n8n.

I currently split my time between a structured **Dev Fellowship** — daily DSA, backend engineering, and system design — and self-driven deep dives into ML/DL, NLP, and computer vision. I care less about shipping something flashy and more about shipping something that's *correct, tested, and built to last*.

I believe in consistency over intensity. Every commit, every DSA problem, and every project here is a small compounding step toward becoming an engineer companies can rely on.
